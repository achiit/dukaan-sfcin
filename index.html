About us

Eight seasons in the same shed

I started at the Sopore mandi 8 years ago, carrying boxes at dawn and learning grades from the older commission agents. The smell of the first apple trucks in September is still my favourite memory.

We have worked hard to keep one thing simple: what you see in the sample is what goes in the box. Growers trust us with their fruit, and buyers keep coming back for that reason.
